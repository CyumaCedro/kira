    About TMB

    Tumor mutational burden counts protein-changing mutations per megabase sequenced. 10 or more per Mb is the FDA's threshold for pembrolizumab eligibility in solid tumors. Treat this number as an estimate: it depends on the panel or exome size and on the variant caller.
